DriveStreamBench

Evaluating User-Conditioned Watch-and-Notify in Streaming Driving Video

Anonymous Author(s)

Abstract

DriveStreamBench is a streaming benchmark for in-vehicle vision-language models. It asks a simple question: while a driving video plays, can the model emit a timely alert only when a user-specified condition is met? We organise this capability into four cognitive levels under a unified streaming evaluation protocol, and provide DriveStream-SFT as a supervised reference baseline.

Primary Metric

AlertScore@τ

AlertScore@τ is the harmonic mean of two components:
  • Hit@τ — fraction of positive sessions whose first non-silent decision lands inside the correctness window around the annotated reference time tref;
  • NSR (Negative Silence Rate) — fraction of negative sessions that stay in NO REPLY throughout.

AlertScore@τ  =  2 · Hit@τ · NSR / (Hit@τ + NSR)
